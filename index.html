The Woods

Past the end of the winding dirt road, across the wide open pasture, the trees started. Every day after school. The woods were calling.

The Tranquil Clearing

A hidden field inside the woods. Tall grass, warm light flooding in. No canopy for the first time in a mile.

The Raging Creek

A valley cut through the middle of everything. Water over flat stones, crawdads ready to be caught.

The Towering Rock

A single boulder at the forest edge. High enough to see through the brush, into the field beyond.

The Enchanted Treehouse

Boards nailed into the old oak. Barely level. Not weatherproof. A kingdom in the woods.
